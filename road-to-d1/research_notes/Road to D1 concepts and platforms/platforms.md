# TikTok vs Instagram Reels vs YouTube Shorts for a cross-posted vertical animated comedy series (late 2025 / 2026)

Context: 1080x1920, ~60-75 s episodes, fully original (procedural 3D animation, original synthesized music, open-model TTS voices for fictional cartoon characters). Research date: 2026-11-23.

**Method caveat:** the primary help centers (support.google.com, support.tiktok.com, tiktok.com/creator-academy) could not be fetched directly from this research session (blocked by network settings). Their content was reached through search-engine excerpts of those pages plus dated press coverage. Wherever a figure comes only from third-party blogs, that is flagged. Re-check the in-app eligibility screens before acting on monetization numbers.

## 1. Max video length and length thresholds that matter

### Takeaway
A 60-75 s episode fits all three platforms. On TikTok it clears the ">1 minute" line that the Creator Rewards Program needs, but only if it is actually over 60 s. Reels are recommended up to 3 min. Shorts run up to 3 min, but any Short over 1 min with a Content ID claim is blocked worldwide.

### Cited Findings
- **YouTube Shorts:** uploads up to 3 minutes have been allowed since **October 15, 2024** (announced Oct 3, 2024 in "Tall updates coming to Shorts"). Before that the limit was 60 s. The change applies to square or taller videos. Videos uploaded before Oct 15, 2024 keep their long-form classification. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/tall-updates-coming-to-shorts/); [YouTube Help: Understand three-minute Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en-GB); [9to5Google, 2024-10-03](https://9to5google.com/2024/10/03/youtube-shorts-3-minutes/)
- YouTube said it would tune recommendations "in the coming months" to surface Shorts up to three minutes long. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/tall-updates-coming-to-shorts/)
- **Shorts over 1 minute with any active Content ID claim (including manual claims) are blocked globally.** — [YouTube Help: Understand three-minute Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)
- **Instagram Reels:** the maximum went from 90 s to **3 minutes in January 2025**, alongside Meta's Breakthrough Bonus push. — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/402749/meta-plugs-5k-breakthrough-bonus-to-attract-ree.html); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-length)
- Reels **over 180 s** get an in-app alert that they won't be recommended to non-followers. They can still reach followers but are unlikely to appear in the Reels tab or Explore. Buffer's analysis of 2.7M Reels found 90 s to 3 min performed best for reach. — [Buffer (last reviewed Nov 2026)](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-length)
- The in-app Instagram camera records up to 90 s. Pre-edited uploads can be up to 3 min. — [Quso](https://quso.ai/research/how-long-are-instagram-reels)
- **Instagram reportedly said in 2024 that it would recommend Reels longer than 90 s** (Social Media Today headline: "Instagram will recommend longer Reels"). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-recommend-longer-reels/737913/)
- **TikTok:** to earn from the Creator Rewards Program, videos must be **longer than 1 minute**. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-creativity-program/); [TikTok Newsroom, 2024-03-18](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en)

### Inferences
- **The 60 s boundary matters on two platforms, in opposite directions.** On TikTok, an episode of 60.0 s or less earns nothing from Creator Rewards, so make episodes at least about 61-62 s; 65-75 s is comfortably safe. On YouTube, once a Short is over 60 s any Content ID claim blocks it, so the music has to be claim-proof (see section 6).
- At 60-75 s, Reels length is not a constraint.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch TikTok's official current upload limits. The brief's figure of 10 min in-app is widely cited, and longer uploads (up to 60 min) exist, but I have no fetched source from this session for either. Neither matters for a 75 s episode.
- I found no official statement that TikTok ranks 1-3 min videos differently from shorter ones. Only the money threshold is documented.

## 2. UI safe zones at 1080x1920

### Takeaway
None of the three platforms publishes an official safe zone for organic posts. The figures that circulate come from tools and agencies and disagree. A conservative union is about **top 240-290 px, bottom 660-672 px, right 190 px, left 48-120 px**.

### Cited Findings
- **YouTube Shorts:** 288 px top, 672 px bottom, 48 px left, 192 px right. Socialync says these come from Google's published vertical video **ad** specs, which makes them the best-sourced of the three platforms. — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/tools/safe-zones/youtube-shorts); [Reap](https://reap.video/blog/short-form-video-safe-zones)
- **Instagram Reels:** 269 px top, 672 px bottom, 65 px each side (14% / 35% / 6%). — [Upload-Post](https://www.upload-post.com/tools/safe-zone-checker/). Contradicted by Adaptlypost: 210 px top, 310 px bottom. — [Adaptlypost](https://adaptlypost.com/blog/social-media-safe-zones-2026-complete-guide)
- **TikTok:** 130 px top, 484 px bottom, 140 px right, 44 px left. — [Bundle.social](https://bundle.social/tools/safe-zone-checker). Contradicted by Upload-Post: 240 px top, 660 px bottom, 120 px each side, with the action buttons taking about 300 px on the right from y≈840 px down. — [Upload-Post](https://www.upload-post.com/tools/safe-zone-checker/). Moda says its TikTok numbers are measured, not official. — [Moda](https://moda.app/tools/story-safe-zone-checker)
- One guide recommends a further 50-100 px buffer beyond the safe-zone edge, because the platforms change their UIs. — [Wildandfreetools 2026 guide](https://wildandfreetools.com/blog/vertical-video-safe-zones-guide-2026/)

### Inferences
- **One master render can serve all three platforms if the important content stays inside about x 120-880, y 290-1250.** That is roughly 760 x 960 px of guaranteed-clean area. Burned-in captions and punchlines belong around y 1000-1250, above the bottom caption and username overlay. Faces should sit in the upper-middle band.
- The bottom roughly 35% is the most dangerous area on all three, because captions, usernames, music tickers and Shorts' subscribe row all go there. The right-hand rail (like, comment, share) is the second danger area.

### Gaps
- No official 2025-2026 organic safe-zone numbers from any platform. Verify by test-posting a grid overlay privately on each app.

## 3. Cross-posting, watermarks and originality / reused-content rules

### Takeaway
Upload the **clean master file** to each platform. Never repost a downloaded TikTok with its watermark. Instagram's originality rules and YouTube's reused/"inauthentic" content rules target accounts that repost **other people's** content or mass-produce near-identical videos. They do not target a creator posting their own original episode on several platforms.

### Cited Findings
- **Instagram (2021):** Reels "visibly recycled from other apps (i.e. contain logos or watermarks)" are made less discoverable to non-followers in places such as the Reels tab. — [Tubefilter, 2021-02-10](https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2551088)
- **Mosseri clarified** that a watermark with your *own* logo does not hurt reach: "if it's your own logo, don't worry about it." Logos from other apps still count against you. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-that-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)
- **Instagram (April 30, 2024):** ranking changed to surface original content and smaller creators. When near-identical reposts exist, the original replaces them in recommendations. Aggregators that post mostly reposted content are penalized, and significantly transformed content is exempt. — [TechCrunch, 2024-04-30](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)
- **Instagram (around April 2026):** the originality policy expanded from Reels to photos and carousels. Accounts are judged on their output over about a month. If most of an account's posts are reposted material, it stops being recommended to non-followers. Reposts carry labels crediting the original creator. Mosseri: "If most of what you post to Instagram is someone else's content, your account is no longer going to be recommendable." — [PetaPixel, 2026-04-30](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [MediaPost](https://mediapost.com/publications/article/414773/instagram-algorithm-update-discourages-reposted-co.html)
- **YouTube (July 15, 2025):** the YPP "repetitious content" policy was renamed **"inauthentic content"**, covering mass-produced or repetitive videos. YouTube described this as a clarification, not a new rule. The "reused content" policy (commentary, compilations, reactions) was unchanged. Reused content can still be monetized if it adds significant original value. — [Tubefilter, 2025-07-03](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/07/03/youtube-crackdown-repetitive-non-original-content/amp/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [Plagiarism Today, 2025-07-08](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/)
- Critics say YouTube's definitions of "original", "reused" and "transformative" are broad and enforced inconsistently. — [Async](https://async.com/blog/youtube-monetization-update/)
- **TikTok:** Creator Rewards excludes videos carrying watermarks, Duets, Stitches, Photo Mode posts and ads or sponsored content. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-creativity-program/)

### Inferences
- **For this series, the real cross-posting risk is the watermark, not "duplication".** Original animation uploaded from the master file is original content on each platform. The account owns it, so Instagram's "replace reposts with the original" logic should not count against it.
- **"Inauthentic / mass-produced" is the one YouTube risk to watch, because the series is procedural and uses TTS.** A templated pipeline producing many near-identical episodes, with the same voice, the same structure and little variation, is the pattern YouTube describes. Distinct stories, writing and characters in each episode are the defence. This is my reading of the policy language, not a ruling.
- A small series logo (the creator's own) is fine on Instagram according to Mosseri.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-2026 source confirming that Instagram's watermark detection is still enforced exactly as in 2021. The April 2026 originality rules suggest the direction has only tightened.
- No official statement on whether uploading the same file to all three platforms on the same day affects distribution on any of them.

## 4. Monetization: numbers and dates

### Takeaway
**YouTube Shorts is the only platform with a durable, documented ad-revenue share**, but it requires 1,000 subscribers plus 10M Shorts views in 90 days. **TikTok Creator Rewards** requires 10k followers, 100k views in 30 days and videos over 1 minute, and pays roughly $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (third-party estimates). **Instagram has no standing per-view pay for Reels.** Bonuses come and go; the latest documented one is the Jan 2025 Breakthrough Bonus.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok Creator Rewards Program (launched March 2024, replacing the Creativity Program)**
- Eligibility: 18+, a personal account in good standing, at least 10,000 followers, at least 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, located in a supported country, videos longer than 1 minute. — [TikTok Newsroom, 2024-03-18](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-creativity-program/); [Toptal (2026)](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program)
- Supported regions in an older list: US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France and Brazil. The list may have changed; check in-app. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-creator-fund/)
- Ineligible: ads or sponsored content, watermarked videos, Duets, Stitches and Photo Mode posts. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-creativity-program/)
- RPM: TikTok publishes no rate. Third-party estimates are **$0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views**, and one dataset of disclosed payouts gives $362-$1,035 per 1M qualified views. US, UK and Western European audiences pay more. — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [Fourthwall](https://fourthwall.com/blog/how-much-does-tiktok-pay); [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/blog/tiktok/tiktok-pay-for-1-million-views)
- Some sources mention a watch-time requirement for qualified views; it is not consistently confirmed. — [ManyChat](https://manychat.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/)

**YouTube Partner Program / Shorts**
- **Lower tier (expanded YPP, June 2023):** 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 public watch hours in 12 months or **3M public Shorts views in 90 days**. Unlocks fan funding (memberships, Super Thanks/Chat/Stickers) and Shopping, but not ad revenue. — [YouTube Help: expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en); [TechCrunch, 2023-06-13](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/13/youtube-is-lowering-the-barrier-to-be-eligible-for-its-monetization-program/.)
- **Ad-revenue tier:** 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 watch hours in 12 months or **10M public Shorts views in 90 days**. — [YouTube Help: expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en); [YouTube creators page](https://www.youtube.com/creators/partner-program)
- Shorts revenue share: creators get **45%** of their allocated share of the Shorts creator pool, which is split by share of eligible views (in effect since Feb 1, 2023). Only a third-party confirmation was found this session. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/monetize-youtube-guide/)
- vidIQ reports that Shorts view thresholds will rise on **Feb 1, 2027** (to 20M for the ad tier). **Unconfirmed on any official YouTube page; treat as rumor until verified.** — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/pt/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-guide)

**Instagram / Meta**
- The Reels Play bonus program ended in **March 2023**. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/meta-is-pausing-bonus-payouts-for-reels-creators-000040951.html); [PetaPixel, 2023-03-10](https://petapixel.com/2023/03/10/meta-cuts-bonus-payouts-for-reels-creators/)
- **Breakthrough Bonus (January 2025):** up to **$5,000** paid over the first 90 days. Requires at least 20 Reels on Facebook and 10 on Instagram per 30-day period, posted on at least 10 separate days. Aimed at US creators with a presence on TikTok or other apps; enrolls creators in **Facebook** Content Monetization. Sources disagree on whether creators who have monetized before qualify. — [Meta creators page](https://creators.facebook.com/breakthrough-bonus/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/402749/meta-plugs-5k-breakthrough-bonus-to-attract-ree.html); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/breakthrough-bonus-meta/)
- Instagram was reported in Jan 2025 to be offering selected TikTokers performance bonuses of up to $50,000/month for posting on Reels (invite-only). — [Tubefilter, 2025-01-22](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/01/22/instagram-performance-bonus-reels-tiktokers/)

### Inferences
- **For a new original animated series, the money order is: YouTube (durable revenue share and fan funding) > TikTok (needs over 1 min and 10k followers; low RPM) > Instagram (bonuses only, mostly via Facebook).** At about $0.40-$1 RPM, 1M qualified TikTok views earns about $400-$1,000.
- Cross-posting the same Reels to **Facebook** is the only route to Meta's per-view money (Facebook Content Monetization). Worth enabling if Facebook Reels is acceptable.

### Gaps
- No primary source for current 2026 TikTok supported-country list or "qualified view" definition (tiktok.com was blocked).
- No 2026 Instagram-only creator payout program found; whether any Reels ads revenue share is live for Instagram (vs Facebook) in 2026 is unconfirmed.
- YouTube Shorts RPM ranges were not researched in sources this session.

## 5. AI disclosure/labeling: TTS voices for fictional cartoon characters

### Takeaway
All three platforms tie mandatory disclosure to **realistic** synthetic content: content that could mislead viewers about real people, places or events. A clearly cartoon, procedurally animated series voiced by open-model TTS for fictional characters appears to fall **outside** mandatory disclosure on YouTube and TikTok, as their policies are worded. Labeling voluntarily carries **no stated reach penalty** on YouTube or TikTok. Instagram's 2026 reach penalty applies to unlabeled *AI-generated-person profiles*, not to cartoons.

### Cited Findings
- **YouTube:** creators must disclose content that is meaningfully altered or synthetically generated **when it seems realistic**. **Clearly unrealistic content such as animation is exempt.** The current page cites "an AI-generated or altered animation of a missile in a fully animated video" as non-realistic. Older versions of the page listed an AI animation of a missile as needing disclosure, so the wording has shifted. Production help (scripts, thumbnails and similar) is exempt. — [YouTube Help: Disclosing use of GenAI content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en-GB); [YouTube Blog, March 2024](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content)
- YouTube: disclosing "won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money". YouTube may add a label itself if content could mislead. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491); [Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/apps-software/youtube-ai-synthetic-content-disclosure)
- **TikTok:** creators must label AI-generated content that contains **realistic** images, audio or video. TikTok auto-labels content made with its own AI effects, or content carrying C2PA Content Credentials, and an auto-label cannot be removed. — [TikTok Support: About AI-generated content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content); [TikTok Newsroom, 2024-05-09](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/advancing-ai-transparency-and-literacy)
- TikTok: turning on the AI-generated label "won't affect the distribution of your video as long as it doesn't violate our Community Guidelines." — [TikTok Support](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content)
- **TikTok, Nov 18-19, 2025:** began testing a viewer-side "AI content" slider (Settings > Content Preferences > Manage Topics) that lets users see less AIGC in For You, plus invisible watermarking. — [TikTok Newsroom, 2025-11-19](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-content?lang=en); [TechCrunch, 2025-11-18](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/18/tiktok-now-lets-you-choose-how-much-ai-generated-content-you-want-to-see/)
- Commentary argues that the slider could indirectly cut reach for content labeled as AI. This is analysis, not policy. — [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/digital-marketing/is-your-brands-ai-content-ready-for-tiktoks-new-labeling-system/)
- **Instagram/Meta:** the post-level **"AI info"** label is applied when C2PA or SynthID markers are detected, or when creators self-disclose. Reports attach no reach penalty to it. On **Aug 31, 2026**, Instagram renamed "AI creator" to an **"AI-generated profile"** label and began limiting reach to non-followers (Reels/Explore) for accounts featuring **AI-generated people** that don't use the label. Accounts that label proactively see no change. — [TechCrunch, 2026-08-31](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2246914/instagram-will-demote-ai-generated-influencers-if-they-dont-clearly-label-their-account/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417580/instagram-limits-creators-reach-for-profiles-with.html)

### Inferences
- **No mandatory label appears to apply to TTS voices of fictional cartoon characters.** The rules target realistic depictions, and especially making *real people* appear to say things they didn't. Do not clone or imitate a real person's voice (e.g. a real coach or announcer), because that moves the content into the realistic or misleading category.
- **Auto-label risk:** procedural 3D and open-model TTS normally write no C2PA or SynthID metadata, so auto-labels are unlikely. If any tool in the pipeline embeds Content Credentials, TikTok and Meta may auto-label the video, and TikTok's label cannot be removed.
- **Where the account could tip into Instagram's "AI-generated profile" rule:** if the series persona is presented as an AI *person* rather than cartoon characters. A clearly cartoon brand should be fine.
- Optional transparency, such as a bio line like "Animated & voiced with synthetic voices", costs nothing on the platforms' stated rules. Weigh it against TikTok's new viewer slider, which only matters if the video carries the platform's AI label.

### Gaps
- No platform document addresses "TTS narration of fictional characters" by name. The conclusion is inferred from the "realistic" criterion.
- Whether Meta's AI info label reduces distribution in practice is unverified (no official statement found either way).

## 6. Music: original vs trending sounds; YouTube Shorts music limits

### Takeaway
Original synthesized music is the **safest** choice for a 60-75 s Short, because no Content ID claim means no global block. Trending sounds may add a short-lived reach lift on TikTok and Reels, according to vendor data. They also add claim and licensing risk, and on YouTube library tracks are limited to 30-90 s within a Short.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube **Shorts audio library** songs carry a special, non-disputable Content ID claim. Most songs can be used for **up to 90 s in a 3-minute Short**; some tracks are capped at **60 or 30 s**. Music from outside the library gets standard Content ID claims and copyright takedowns. — [YouTube Help: Music eligibility for Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13486873?hl=en); [YouTube Help: Manage Shorts as a rights holder](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13053317)
- Shorts **over 1 minute with any active Content ID claim are blocked globally**. — [YouTube Help 15424877](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)
- Tracks from the YouTube **Audio Library** (royalty-free) do not receive a Content ID claim. — [YouTube Help: Learn about Content ID claims](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6013276)
- Trending sounds: vendor blogs report a reach lift of **+15-25%** for sounds caught in their growth phase, up to **+47%** in other claims. Original audio starts about **22% lower** in reach but builds stronger long-term association. Trends last about 1-3 weeks. None of these sources publish a verifiable methodology. — [ttsvibes insights](https://insights.ttsvibes.com/tiktok-original-sound-vs-trending-sound-performance/); [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/guides/growth/tiktok-sounds-music-distribution/)
- Some trending audio is not available to business accounts because of music licensing restrictions. — [Socialk.it](https://socialk.it/en/blog/tiktok-sounds-strategy)

### Inferences
- **Upload the episode with its own mixed soundtrack on every platform.** On TikTok and Instagram it becomes an "original sound" named after the series, which other people can reuse. That is a free discovery loop for a recurring theme song.
- **Avoid in-app trending songs on YouTube Shorts over 60 s.** Any claim on a 61 s+ Short blocks it worldwide. In-app library songs are allowed, but they are capped at 30-90 s and they replace or overlay the dialogue mix.
- If the music is ever registered with a distributor or Content ID (for example, releasing the theme on Spotify through a distributor that enrolls tracks in Content ID), whitelist your own channel first. Otherwise your own Shorts over 60 s could be claimed and blocked. This is an inference from the blocking rule.
- On TikTok and Reels, a trending sound at very low volume under the dialogue is a common creator tactic. I found **no evidence** on whether it helps.

### Gaps
- Instagram's specific rules on original audio vs licensed music for creator vs business accounts in 2026 were not confirmed.
- No source on the low-volume trending-sound tactic.

## 7. Captions, hashtags and posting conventions per platform

### Takeaway
Instagram has hard-capped hashtags at **5 per post** since Dec 2025, and it now ranks on caption keywords and on-screen text. TikTok and YouTube have no comparable caps found. Burned-in captions inside the safe zone work everywhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Instagram reduced the hashtag limit from 30 to 5** per post and Reel, rolling out gradually from **December 2025**, after about a year of testing a 3-hashtag limit. Mosseri says hashtags are no longer a main reach driver; caption keywords, on-screen text and visuals matter more. — [Business Today, 2025-12-19](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- Threads allows one hashtag per post. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)

### Inferences
- **Instagram:** 3-5 specific hashtags (e.g. #animation #footballcomedy #D3football). Write a keyword-rich first caption line, because Instagram search reads captions.
- **TikTok:** the caption doubles as search text. Put "football cartoon" style keywords plus a few niche hashtags in it.
- **YouTube Shorts:** the title is the main metadata. #Shorts is not required for 3-minute vertical classification (classification is by aspect ratio and length per the help page above). Add the series to a playlist to make bingeing easier.

### Gaps
- **Posting times:** no platform-official 2025-2026 guidance found. Third-party "best time" studies exist but were not gathered this session.
- TikTok and YouTube hashtag count limits were not confirmed this session.

## 8. Synthesis: comparison table and cross-posting playbook

### Takeaway
Post one clean master to all three platforms, with the episode running about 62-75 s and original music. Keep text inside a shared safe zone. Don't add AI labels unless realistic people or voices appear. Pursue YouTube first for money, TikTok as a secondary earner once over 10k followers, and Instagram for reach (and Facebook for Meta money).
